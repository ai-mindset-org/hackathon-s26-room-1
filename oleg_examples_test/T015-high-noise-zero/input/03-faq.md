# ECHO-ZERO records portal FAQ
Last reviewed: March 5, 2028
Portal section: ECHO-ZERO/FAQ

## Why do dates look different in two exports?
The New York portal keeps the US order month/day/year.
The Rotterdam portal keeps the EU order day/month/year.
For example, New York 04/05/2028 means April 5, while Rotterdam 04/05/2028 means 4 May.
The region label beside the export is the deciding context.
The portal does not rewrite the original display in downloaded text.

## Why is a canceled room hold still searchable?
Calendar history keeps the declined or canceled entry with its original date and organizer.
This helps explain access logs, catering estimates, and empty room periods.
A replacement appears under its own event identifier if the organizer later books one.
Old search results can therefore contain several dates for a single room name.
The current calendar view remains the source for whether a booking exists.

## What does the idea channel contain?
The ECHO-ZERO/Ideas channel is a place for questions, comparisons, sketches, and reactions.
A typical post asks, Would a pocket route card be easier to read than the wall map?
Another example is, If a sponsor joins, could the lobby trial return for one week?
Replies often discuss paper sizes, colours, costs, and examples from earlier pilots.
The channel archive remains searchable after a proposal is declined or simply fades out.

## How are historical reports displayed?
Reports retain the wording used when a programme closed.
Completed surveys and rejected options remain in the index because they support later audits.
A report publication date can be months after the work described in its body.
The portal labels both the reporting period and the publication date.
Revision numbers such as R4 or 2.7 identify documents rather than project phases.

## What is the number beside an automated summary?
The percentage is the service estimate that related words belong in one topic cluster.
It can rise when a project name repeats in quoted history or calendar metadata.
A date may be the timestamp of the source message, the export, or the text being quoted.
The source links under the summary show which interpretation is available.
The service processes text at 02:10 UTC and retains seven daily snapshots.

## Where are sample templates stored?
The handbook includes placeholders for topic, question, alternatives, decision, owner, and target.
Training pages use ECHO-ZERO/Sample so they cannot be confused with named programme folders.
Example values such as 06/01/2028 or EUR 2.500,00 illustrate formatting in the portal preview.
The template library version is ECHO-ZERO/Templates/3.4.
Support hours are 08:30-17:00 Eastern and 09:00-17:30 Central European Time.
